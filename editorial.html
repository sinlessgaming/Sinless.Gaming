Editorial & copyright policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Discover on SINLESS GG brings together news, trailers, rumours and leaks about games, sport, movies, music, tech and more from publishers around the world. We help people find stories fast and always send them to the original source.

What we show

  • Headline, a short preview and a link. We show the publisher's headline, a short preview of up to about 200 characters, the publisher's name and a link to the full story. We never copy whole articles.
  • No source images. Stories use our own topic cards. Videos are shown with YouTube's own player and thumbnail, so views count for the creator.
  • Community posts (for example from public subreddits) show only the headline and a link.
  • GG Brief is a short summary written in our own words by an AI model, based on the sources listed under the story. It can make mistakes, so every brief links to the originals.

Labels on every story

🟢 Official: from the company, league, club or studio itself.
🔵 Reported: from an established news publisher.
🟡 Rumour: not confirmed. Treat it with care.
💧 Leak: information that has not been officially released. We link to reports about leaks; we never host leaked files.
👥 Community: posted by fans, not checked by us.
🎬 Video: an embedded video from the creator's own channel.

When several sources report the same story we merge them into one card and show how many sources there are. The timeline on a card shows how a story moved, for example from rumour to official.

What we never include

  • Pirated games, films, music or modified apps, or links to them.
  • Betting tips, odds promotion or crypto trading signals.
  • Private gossip about people who are not public figures, or anything that exposes someone's private life.
  • Re-uploads of other people's videos.
  • Hate, harassment, adult content or anything illegal.

Spoilers

Stories that may contain spoilers are marked ⚠️ Spoilers and their preview is blurred until you tap it.

Corrections

If a label is wrong (for example a rumour that is now confirmed or denied) the card updates when sources report it. If you see a mistake, use Contact below.

Publishers and creators: removal within 24 hours

If you own a story, video or feed that appears on SINLESS GG and want it changed or removed, email sinlessggofficial@gmail.com with the link to the item and your feed or channel. We act within 24 hours and can stop showing your feed entirely.
