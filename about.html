About Sinless GG

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Sinless GG is a free social app for gamers and fans: football, mobile and console games, wrestling, movies & TV, anime and books, all in one place.

Play. Host and join tournaments and leagues: knockouts, World Cup style, Champions League style and full league tables, with automatic brackets and tables.

Watch. Share clips and photos, follow live football scores, and catch the latest news and gist from football, gaming, movies, anime, books and wrestling.

Connect. Join squads with channels, roles and moderation, chat in lobbies, send direct messages, run polls and match predictions, and climb the weekly creator tables.

Who we are

Sinless GG is an independent project built in Ghana 🇬🇭 for players and fans everywhere. Our goal is a friendly, fair place to share what you love, find teammates and compete.

Keeping it safe

Every post can be reported. Our admin team reviews reports, removes content that breaks our Terms, and can suspend accounts. Squad owners and admins can also mute, kick and ban members in their own squads.

Advertising

Sinless GG is free to use. To keep it running, we show ads. Ads from businesses are clearly marked “Sponsored”, and we may also show ads from Google. See our Privacy Policy for details.
