How SINLESS GG tournaments work: a complete player guide

SINLESS GG Guides · Updated 28 October 2026

Tournaments are the heart of SINLESS GG. Whether you play FC Mobile, eFootball, DLS or another game, this guide walks you through everything from joining to lifting the trophy, so you never miss a match or lose points for a simple mistake.

In this guide: Joining · Finding your fixtures · Playing your match · Reporting the score · Deadlines and forfeits · Disputes · Titles

1. Joining a tournament

Open the Tournaments page and browse what is open. Each card shows the game, the format (league, knockout or groups), how many places are left and when it starts. Tap a tournament to read its rules before you join.

  • Read the rules first. Hosts set match length, team strength limits, and what happens if you do not play on time. When you join, you accept those rules.
  • Add your in-game name or ID. Your opponent needs it to find and invite you. It is locked when you join so nobody can swap accounts halfway through.
  • Squad tournaments are only for members of that squad. You can join the squad first, then the tournament.

2. Finding your fixtures

Every running tournament has a Today's Fixtures board. Your own matches are marked with a ⭐ so you can spot them instantly. Use the tabs to switch between Today, Tomorrow, This week and Unplayed.

If the host has set play times, every match gets its own 10 to 15 minute slot inside the host's hours, and nobody is booked for two matches at once. You will also get a reminder in the morning and another one 30 minutes before your slot.

Tip: tap any player's name on the board to see their fixtures, their recent form and your head-to-head record against them.

3. Playing your match

  1. Message your opponent early. Agree on a time inside the match day.
  2. Use the in-game name from the tournament to send or accept the friendly match invite.
  3. Play by the host's rules. If something goes wrong (a disconnect, for example), agree on a fair replay before anyone reports a score.
  4. Take a screenshot of the final score. It is your proof if there is a disagreement.

4. Reporting the score

Open the match from the board and enter the score exactly as it ended. Attach your screenshot if the tournament asks for proof. Depending on the host's settings, the result is either approved straight away or waits for the host to confirm it.

Once a result is approved, the league table updates instantly, a short result line appears in the squad chat, and you get a result card you can share to your status.

5. Deadlines and forfeits

Each match has a play-by time. If you cannot make it, ask your opponent and the host to move the match before the deadline. When a deadline passes, the host decides what happens:

  • One player forfeits: the player who did not show up loses 1–0.
  • Both forfeit: in leagues, the host can record a loss for both players, so nobody gets points.
  • Extra time: if both of you asked for more time, the host can give two more days.
Leaving early? If you leave a squad while you still have unplayed matches in its tournaments, those matches are forfeited. Finish your games first.

6. Disputes

If you and your opponent report different scores, the match is marked as disputed and goes to the host. Hosts can see every report, the screenshots and the full match history, and every decision is logged with a reason. Be honest and keep your proof. Fair play protects everyone.

7. Titles and the power ranking

Winners receive a title that stays on their profile forever, and the current champion of each competition gets a gold label next to their name. Results also feed the power ranking, which works like international football rankings: wins in top leagues, cups, the Champions League and finals count for more, and trophies add bonus points. Players are grouped into tiers from S down to D.

Titles won by cheating can be removed by the host or by SINLESS GG staff. Play clean and your trophies stay yours.
