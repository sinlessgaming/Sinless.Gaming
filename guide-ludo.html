Ludo rules and strategy: how to win more games

SINLESS GG Guides · Updated 28 October 2026

Ludo looks like a game of pure luck, but good players win far more often than bad ones. Luck decides each roll. Strategy decides what you do with it. Here are the rules we use on SINLESS GG and the habits that make the biggest difference.

The rules in one minute

  • Each player has four tokens that start in their home base.
  • You need to roll a 6 to bring a token out onto the start square.
  • Rolling a 6 also gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row and your turn ends.
  • Landing exactly on an opponent's token sends it back to their base. Coloured start squares and star squares are safe, so nobody can be captured there.
  • Tokens travel around the board, then up the coloured home path. You need the exact number to finish.
  • The first player to bring all four tokens home wins.

Strategy that actually works

1. Get more than one token out early

With only one token on the board, every roll has to move that token, even into danger. Two or three tokens out gives you choices, and choices win games.

2. Use safe squares

Before you move, look at where each token would land. If one move lands on a safe square and the other leaves you one step in front of an opponent, choose safety.

3. Count the danger zone

An opponent can only capture you if they are 1 to 6 squares behind you. Try not to stop within six squares in front of an enemy token unless you are on a safe square.

4. Capture when it matters

Sending back a token that is nearly home is worth much more than capturing one that just came out. When you have a choice, hit the most advanced enemy token.

5. Do not race one token home alone

Rushing a single token home feels good, but the other three are still in danger. A steady, spread-out approach is stronger.

6. Block with pairs

Keeping two of your tokens close together makes it harder for opponents to move past safely and gives you a counter-attack if they get close.

Playing online on SINLESS GG

You can play Ludo against the AI, with friends on one phone, or online with real players using a room code. Online games show whose turn it is, and if the connection drops for a moment, the board heals itself so every player sees the same position.

Points: a win against the AI earns 5 points, a win against one real player earns 20, and every extra real player in the game adds 10 more.
Play fair: be patient when it is not your turn and say GG at the end. Friendly games keep players coming back.
