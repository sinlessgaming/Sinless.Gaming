Penalty King: how to score more and beat the keeper

SINLESS GG Guides · Updated 28 October 2026

Penalty King is one of the quickest games on SINLESS GG: tap where you want to shoot and try to beat the keeper. One miss and it is over, so every shot counts. These tips will help you build longer streaks and climb the weekly leaderboard.

How the game works

  • Tap anywhere in the goal to choose where your shot goes.
  • The keeper dives to try to save it. If the ball goes in, your score goes up and you shoot again.
  • If the keeper saves it, or the shot goes wide, the game ends.
  • Your best score is saved, and signed-in players appear on the weekly leaderboard.

Where to aim

Corners are your friend

Shots near the corners are much harder to reach than shots near the middle. Aim a little inside the posts rather than right on the line, so a small slip does not send the ball wide.

Mix up your sides

If you always shoot to the same corner, you become predictable. Change sides often and do not fall into a simple left-right pattern either.

Use height

High shots near the top corners are the hardest to save. Low shots near the posts are safer. Use both.

Building a long streak

  1. Slow down. There is no rush between shots. Take a breath and choose your spot.
  2. Do not get greedy. The very edge of the goal is risky. A shot slightly inside the post is almost as hard to save and much less likely to go wide.
  3. Reset after a big score. Long streaks make players nervous. Treat every shot like the first one.

Play with friends

Penalty King also works online against real players. Each player takes their shots and the higher score wins. Beating a real player earns 20 points, which is four times more than a win against the AI.

Challenge your squad: share your best score in your squad chat and see who can beat it before the weekly leaderboard resets.
