FC Mobile tips: 12 habits that win more matches

SINLESS GG Guides · Updated 28 October 2026

Most head-to-head matches are not won by the player with the highest team rating. They are won by the player who makes fewer mistakes. These habits are simple, they work at every level, and you can practise all of them in your next game.

Before kick-off

1. Pick one formation and learn it properly

Jumping between formations every week makes you slower, because you never learn where your players will be. Choose a balanced shape you like, such as a 4-3-3 or 4-2-3-1, and stick with it for a few weeks. You will start to know your passing lanes without thinking.

2. Put your fastest defenders in the middle

Through balls down the middle cause most goals in casual matches. Pace at centre-back gives you time to recover when your line gets beaten.

3. Check stamina and positions

Tired players react late. Before a tournament match, make sure your best eleven are rested and every player is in a position they are comfortable in.

Attacking

4. Pass, then move

Holding the ball and running into three defenders rarely works. Quick passes pull defenders out of position. Two or three short passes followed by one forward pass is a reliable pattern.

5. Shoot from good positions, not from anywhere

Long shots feel exciting but miss most of the time. Work the ball into the box, aim for the far corner and keep your shots low when the keeper is close.

6. Use the width

If the middle is crowded, switch play to a winger and cross early or cut the ball back to the edge of the box. Cut-backs are one of the most effective ways to score in any football game.

7. Learn two skill moves, not twenty

One move to beat a defender and one to change direction are enough. Use them at the right moment, near the box, instead of in your own half.

Defending

8. Do not chase with your centre-backs

Pulling a centre-back out to press leaves a gap behind him. Jockey, stay between the attacker and your goal, and let your midfielders press.

9. Time your tackles

Wait for the attacker to take a heavy touch, then tackle. Rushing in gives away fouls and penalties.

Mind-set

10. Stay calm after conceding

Many matches are lost in the five minutes after a goal, when players rush. Keep the ball for a while, find your rhythm, then attack.

11. Watch your own replays

After a loss, look at the goals you conceded. Usually the same mistake appears again and again, and that is the one to fix first.

12. Respect your opponent
